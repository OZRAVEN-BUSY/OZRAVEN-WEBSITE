OZRAVEN WEBSITE — QUICK START

1. Extract the ZIP file.
2. Open the extracted folder.
3. Double-click index.html to preview the website in a browser.
4. Keep the assets folder beside index.html.

CUSTOMISE
- Replace product names, prices and descriptions in script.js (the `products` object).
- Replace BUSINESS_EMAIL in script.js with your real business email.
- Replace the sample product art in index.html/style.css with real product previews as your catalogue grows.
- Update the about and services copy in index.html to match your actual business.

FUNCTIONALITY
- Responsive navigation and mobile menu
- Product category filters
- Product detail popups
- Add/remove products and quantity controls
- Shopping bag saved in browser localStorage
- Demo checkout and contact form that open a pre-filled email after you set BUSINESS_EMAIL

IMPORTANT
This is a front-end website. It does not process payments or automatically deliver files. To accept real orders, connect a payment provider and a secure backend/order workflow. Do not collect or store payment card details in this static site.
